# Cooking Dinner for Friends

On Saturday Nadia invited four friends to dinner. She wanted to cook something
special, so she chose a new recipe. She has never tried it before.

She spent the morning at the market and bought fresh ingredients: chicken, red
peppers, onions and a lot of spices. She also bought a bottle of wine.

By two o'clock she was in the kitchen. She followed the recipe carefully, step
by step. First she chopped the vegetables. Then she put the chicken in a bowl
with oil, lemon and garlic and left it there for an hour.

While she was waiting, she cleaned the apartment and set the table. She used
her best plates and put two candles in the middle. It looked simple and warm.

At six o'clock she started cooking the main dish. Soon the whole apartment
smelled wonderful.

Then she opened the cupboard and stopped. The rice was finished. She had
chicken, sauce, vegetables — and nothing to serve them with.

She sent a message to Patricia, who lives in the same building: "Do you have
rice? Any rice? I am desperate."

Two minutes later Patricia was at the door with a full bag of rice, laughing.

At half past seven the guests arrived. Kevin brought dessert and Patricia
brought more wine than anybody could drink.

"Something smells amazing," Kevin said.

"I hope it tastes as good as it smells," Nadia answered.

They sat down together and ate slowly. The chicken was a success. Everybody
asked for the recipe, and Nadia felt quite proud of herself.

After dinner they stayed at the table for hours, talking and drinking coffee.
Nobody wanted the evening to finish.

"We have to do this every month," Patricia said.

Everybody agreed immediately.

## New Words

- **invite** — to ask somebody to come to your home or an event
- **recipe** — instructions that tell you how to cook a dish
- **ingredient** — one of the foods you need to make a dish
- **spice** — a strong-tasting powder or seed you add to food
- **chop** — to cut food into small pieces
- **garlic** — a strong white vegetable used in small amounts in cooking
- **cupboard** — a piece of kitchen furniture with doors where you keep food
- **desperate** — needing something very badly
- **dessert** — the sweet food at the end of a meal
- **success** — when something works well
