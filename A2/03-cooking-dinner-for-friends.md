# Cooking Dinner for Friends

On Saturday, Nadia invited four friends to her apartment for dinner. She wanted to cook something special, so she decided to try a new recipe.

She spent the morning at the market, buying fresh ingredients. She bought chicken, peppers, onions, and a lot of spices. She also picked up a bottle of wine for the evening.

By two o'clock, Nadia was in the kitchen. She followed the recipe carefully, step by step. First, she chopped the vegetables. Then, she marinated the chicken for one hour.

While she waited, she cleaned her apartment and set the table. She used her best plates and lit two candles in the middle of the table. It looked elegant and cozy.

At six o'clock, she started cooking the main dish. The kitchen soon smelled amazing. However, halfway through, she realized she was missing an important ingredient: garlic!

She quickly texted her neighbor. "Do you have any garlic? I'm cooking and I forgot to buy some!"

Her neighbor laughed and brought her three cloves of garlic a few minutes later. Nadia was relieved.

At seven thirty, her friends arrived. Tom brought dessert, and Maria brought a salad. Everyone helped set up the last details.

"Something smells delicious," said Tom as he walked in.

"I hope it tastes as good as it smells," Nadia replied nervously.

They all sat down together. The chicken dish was a success. Everyone asked for the recipe, and Nadia felt proud of her cooking.

After dinner, they stayed at the table for hours, talking, laughing, and drinking coffee. Nobody wanted the evening to end.

"We should do this again next month," suggested Maria.

Everyone agreed immediately. Nadia smiled. Cooking for her friends wasn't just about the food — it was about spending time together.
