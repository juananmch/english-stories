# A Surprise Visit

Diana hadn't seen her best friend, Claire, in almost a year. Claire had moved to another country for work, and they only talked through video calls.

One weekend, Diana decided to do something special. She booked a flight without telling Claire and planned to surprise her.

She arrived at Claire's city on a Saturday morning. She was nervous but excited as she took a taxi to Claire's apartment. She had gotten the address from Claire's roommate, who agreed to help with the surprise.

When Diana arrived, Claire's roommate opened the door and winked at her. "She's in the kitchen," she whispered.

Diana quietly walked toward the kitchen. Claire was making coffee, completely unaware of what was happening.

"Excuse me, do you have any sugar?" Diana asked, disguising her voice.

Claire turned around, confused for a second. Then her eyes widened. "Wait... Diana?! Is that really you?"

"Surprise!" Diana shouted, laughing.

Claire dropped her spoon and ran to hug her. "I can't believe this! What are you doing here?"

"I missed you too much," Diana admitted. "I had to come see you."

They spent the whole day together, catching up on everything that had happened since Claire moved. Claire showed her around the city: the markets, the parks, and her favorite little café.

In the evening, they cooked dinner together in Claire's apartment, just like they used to do back home. They talked for hours, sharing stories and laughing about old memories.

"I still can't believe you flew all this way just to surprise me," Claire said, shaking her head.

"You're worth it," Diana replied. "Besides, I needed a vacation too."

Before Diana left on Monday, they promised to visit each other more often. "Distance doesn't change our friendship," Claire said. "But please, next time, warn me before showing up in my kitchen!"

They both laughed. Even though the visit was short, it reminded them both how special their friendship really was.
