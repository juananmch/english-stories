# Moving to a New Apartment

Last month Jennifer moved to a new apartment. Her old one was too small, and
after four years there she wanted more space.

She looked online for three weeks and visited five apartments before she found
the right one. It has two bedrooms, a big kitchen and a small balcony with a
view of the street. The rent is a little higher than before, but she can afford
it.

On the day of the move, her friend Robert and two other friends helped her.
They rented a truck and carried boxes all morning. It was hard work, and by
lunchtime everybody was tired.

"Thank you so much," Jennifer said. "I really could not do this alone."

"That is what friends are for," Robert answered.

When they finished, Jennifer ordered pizza for everybody. They sat on the floor
and ate, because there was no furniture in the living room yet. It felt strange,
but they laughed a lot.

During the first week Jennifer unpacked her boxes slowly. She bought a cheap
sofa and a small table for the kitchen. She also painted one wall light blue.

The neighborhood is quite different from her old one. There is a park two
minutes away and a market every Sunday morning. Jennifer walks there early and
buys fresh vegetables and bread.

She also met her neighbors. An older couple lives next door. They knocked on
her door in the first week and brought her a plate of cookies. Jennifer thought
that was very kind.

At night the apartment is sometimes too quiet. Her old street was noisy, and
she is still getting used to the silence.

"I think I made the right decision," she told her mother on the phone. "It is
bigger, it is brighter, and I feel safe here."

## New Words

- **balcony** — a small floor outside a window where you can stand
- **view** — what you can see from a window or a high place
- **rent** — the money you pay every month to live in a place
- **afford** — to have enough money for something
- **truck** — a big vehicle for carrying heavy things
- **furniture** — tables, chairs, beds and other big things in a home
- **unpack** — to take things out of boxes or bags
- **neighborhood** — the part of a city around your home
- **couple** — two people who live together as partners
- **decision** — what you choose after you think about something
