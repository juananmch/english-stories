# Moving to a New Apartment

Last month, Jennifer moved to a new apartment. Her old apartment was too small, so she decided to look for something bigger.

She spent three weeks searching online. She visited five apartments before she found the right one. It has two bedrooms, a big kitchen, and a balcony with a nice view of the street.

On moving day, Jennifer's brother and two friends helped her. They rented a small truck and carried boxes all morning. It was hard work, and everyone was tired by lunchtime.

"Thank you so much for your help," Jennifer said. "I couldn't do this alone."

"That's what friends are for," her brother answered, smiling.

After they finished, Jennifer ordered pizza for everyone. They sat on the floor because there was no furniture yet. It felt a little strange, but also fun.

During the first week, Jennifer unpacked her boxes slowly. She bought a new sofa and a small table for the kitchen. She also painted one wall in the living room a light blue color.

The new neighborhood is different from her old one. There is a park nearby, and a farmers market every Sunday. Jennifer likes to walk there in the morning and buy fresh vegetables.

She also met her neighbors. An older couple lives next door. They introduced themselves the first week and brought her some homemade cookies. Jennifer thought that was very kind.

At night, sometimes the apartment feels too quiet because she is used to her old street, which was noisier. But she is slowly getting used to her new home.

"I think I made the right decision," Jennifer told her mother on the phone. "This apartment is bigger, brighter, and the neighborhood is safer too."

Her mother agreed. "You deserve a nice place after working so hard."

Jennifer smiled. For the first time in a long time, she felt settled and happy in her own space.
