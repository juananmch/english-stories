# A Visit to the Doctor

For two days, Kevin had a bad headache and a sore throat. He also felt very tired, so he decided to see a doctor.

He called the clinic in the morning and got an appointment for that afternoon. He was a little nervous because he doesn't like hospitals or clinics very much.

At the clinic, a nurse checked his temperature and blood pressure before the appointment. "The doctor will see you in a few minutes," she said.

Soon, Dr. Patel called him into her office. "What's the problem?" she asked kindly.

Kevin explained his symptoms. "I have a headache, a sore throat, and I feel exhausted all the time."

Dr. Patel listened carefully and then examined him. She checked his throat and listened to his breathing with a stethoscope.

"It looks like you have a common cold," she said. "It's not serious, but you need to rest."

She gave him some advice: drink plenty of water, sleep more, and avoid cold drinks. She also wrote a prescription for some medicine to help with the pain.

"Take one pill twice a day, after meals," she explained. "If you don't feel better in a week, come back to see me."

Kevin thanked her and went to the pharmacy near the clinic. The pharmacist gave him the medicine and explained the instructions again.

That evening, Kevin stayed home instead of going to the gym. He made himself a cup of tea and went to bed early.

The next morning, he already felt a little better. He decided to take two days off work to fully recover.

By the end of the week, Kevin felt completely healthy again. He was thankful he went to the doctor instead of ignoring his symptoms.

"It's always better to take care of your health early," he told his sister on the phone. She agreed completely.
