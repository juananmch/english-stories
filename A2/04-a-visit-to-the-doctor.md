# A Visit to the Doctor

For two days Kevin had a bad headache and a sore throat. He also felt tired all
the time, so on Wednesday morning he decided to see a doctor.

He called the clinic and got an appointment for that afternoon. Kevin does not
like clinics very much, so he was a little nervous.

At the clinic a nurse checked his temperature before the appointment. "The
doctor will see you in a few minutes," she said.

Soon Doctor Patel called him into her office.

"So, what is the problem?" she asked.

Kevin described his symptoms: the headache, the sore throat, and the tiredness.

Doctor Patel listened carefully and then examined him. She looked at his throat
and listened to his breathing.

"It is a bad cold," she said. "It is not serious, but you have to rest. You
cannot work like this."

She gave him some advice: drink a lot of water, sleep more, and stay warm. Then
she wrote a prescription for something for the pain.

"Take one pill twice a day, after meals," she explained. "If you do not feel
better in a week, come back."

Kevin thanked her and walked to the pharmacy next to the clinic. The pharmacist
gave him the medicine and explained the instructions again.

That evening he stayed home instead of going out. He made himself a hot drink
and went to bed at nine o'clock.

The next morning he already felt a little better, but he took two days off work
anyway.

His friend Nadia, who is a nurse, called him on Friday.

"Are you resting?" she asked. "Really resting, not working from your sofa?"

"Really resting," Kevin laughed.

By the weekend he felt completely healthy again. He was glad he went to the
doctor instead of waiting.

## New Words

- **sore** — painful when you touch it or use it
- **throat** — the part inside your neck that you use to swallow and speak
- **appointment** — a time you arrange to see a doctor or another person
- **serious** — bad enough to worry about
- **symptom** — a sign that shows you are sick
- **examine** — to look at somebody carefully to find a problem
- **advice** — words that tell somebody what is good to do
- **prescription** — a paper from a doctor that lets you get medicine
- **pill** — a small hard piece of medicine that you swallow
- **pharmacy** — the store where you buy medicine
