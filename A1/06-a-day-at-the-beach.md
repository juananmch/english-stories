# A Day at the Beach

Last Sunday Carlos and Ana went to the beach with their children, Diego and
Sofia.

They left home at eight o'clock in the morning. The beach is two hours from
their city, so they went by car. Diego and Sofia slept in the car.

When they arrived, the sun was very hot and the sky was blue. There were many
people on the sand, but they found a good place near the water.

Ana put a big towel on the sand. Carlos and the children went to the sea. The
water was cold at first, but after five minutes it was perfect.

Diego can swim very well, so he swam with his father. Sofia is only five years
old and she cannot swim, so she played near the water with her feet.

At one o'clock they were hungry. Ana made sandwiches with cheese and tomato,
and they ate them under a big umbrella. Then Carlos bought four ice creams. The
children were very happy.

In the afternoon Sofia and Diego made a big house of sand. It was not a good
house, because the sand was dry, but they laughed a lot.

At six o'clock the sun was low and the wind was cold. It was time to go home.

"I do not want to go!" Sofia said.

"We are going to come again in August," Ana said. "I promise."

In the car the children slept again. Carlos looked at them and he was happy. It
was a very good day.

## New Words

- **beach** — the place with sand next to the sea
- **sand** — the very small yellow stones on a beach
- **sea** — the big water between two countries
- **swim** — to move your body in water
- **towel** — cloth you use to get dry
- **umbrella** — you open it over your head for sun or rain
- **ice cream** — very cold sweet food, often white or brown
- **dry** — not wet, with no water in it
