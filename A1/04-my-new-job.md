# My New Job

Today is Monday. It is my first day at a new job. My name is Miguel and I am 31 years old.

I am nervous, but also happy. I wake up early, at six o'clock. I have a shower and eat breakfast. I don't eat much because I am nervous.

I put on my new shirt and my black trousers. I look in the mirror. "You can do this," I say to myself.

I leave home at seven thirty. The office is in the city center. I take the train because it is fast.

The train is busy. Many people go to work at this time. I stand near the door and look out of the window.

I arrive at the office at eight fifteen. The building is big and modern. I feel small next to it.

A woman is waiting for me at the door. Her name is Sandra. She is the manager.

"Welcome, Miguel," she says with a smile. "Please, come with me."

She shows me the office. There are many desks and computers. My desk is next to a window. I like that.

Sandra introduces me to my new co-workers. Their names are Paul, Rita, and Tom. They seem nice and friendly.

"Do you have any questions?" asks Sandra.

"Not now," I say, "but maybe later."

At lunch, Rita invites me to eat with her. We talk about our lives. She has worked here for three years.

My first day is long, but not bad. I learn a lot of new things. I am tired at the end of the day, but I feel good.

I think I am going to like this job.
