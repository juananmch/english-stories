# My Family

I want to tell you about my family. My name is Carlos. I am 35 years old.

I have a wife. Her name is Ana. She is a teacher. She works at a primary school. She likes her job very much because she loves children.

We have two children. Our son is called Diego. He is eight years old. Our daughter is called Sofia. She is five years old. Diego likes football. Sofia likes to draw.

We live in a house with a small garden. My father helps us in the garden. He is retired now, but he was a taxi driver for thirty years.

My mother is a good cook. Every Sunday, we visit my parents' house for lunch. My mother cooks a big meal: soup, meat, and salad. After lunch, we drink coffee and eat cake.

I also have a brother. His name is Pablo. He lives in another city with his girlfriend. He is a doctor. We don't see him often, but we talk on the phone every week.

My family is very important to me. We are not rich, but we are happy together. On Sundays, the children play in the garden with their grandfather. My mother and Ana talk in the kitchen. I watch football with my father and my brother, when he visits.

Family time is my favorite time of the week.
