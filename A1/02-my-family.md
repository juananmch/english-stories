# My Family

Hello. My name is Carlos and I am 35 years old. I want to talk about my family.

My wife is Ana. She is a teacher at a school in our city. She loves her work
because she likes children very much.

We have two children. Our son is Diego. He is eight years old. Our daughter is
Sofia. She is five years old. Diego likes soccer and Sofia likes to draw.

We live in a small house. There is a garden behind the house. In the summer we
eat dinner in the garden.

My father lives near us. He is 68 years old. He is a grandfather now, but he
was a taxi driver for thirty years. He helps us in the garden every week.

My mother is a very good cook. Every Sunday we go to my parents' house. My
mother makes soup, meat and salad. After lunch we drink coffee and we eat cake.

I also have a brother. His name is Pablo and he is a doctor. He lives in
another city, so we do not see him every week. But we talk on the phone.

We are not rich, but we are happy. For me, Sunday is the best day of the week,
because all my family is together.

## New Words

- **wife** — the woman a man is married to
- **son** — somebody's boy child
- **daughter** — somebody's girl child
- **garden** — the piece of land next to a house with plants in it
- **grandfather** — the father of your mother or your father
- **doctor** — a person who helps you when you are sick
- **together** — with other people, in the same place
- **rich** — with a lot of money

## Questions

1. How old are Carlos's children?
2. Where is the garden?
3. What was Carlos's father's job?
4. What does Carlos's mother make on Sunday?
5. Why is Sunday the best day for Carlos?
