# Learning English

Laura is learning English. She started her class six months ago, and now she
studies two days every week.

Her class is on Tuesday and Thursday, from seven to nine in the evening. She
goes after work, so sometimes she is very tired. But she likes the class a lot.

There are twelve students in Laura's class. They are from many countries:
Brazil, China, Poland and Mexico. Her teacher is Mr. Brown and he is from
England.

"Do not be afraid of mistakes," Mr. Brown always says. "Mistakes are normal.
People who do not make mistakes do not learn."

In class they learn new words every day. They also speak with the other
students. This is the difficult part for Laura, because she is quiet.

At home Laura studies for thirty minutes every day. She has a small book with
all her new words, and she reads it on the bus. She also watches films in
English with the words on the television.

Six months ago English was very difficult for her. Now she can talk about her
family, her work and her day. She can read simple books too.

Last week something very good happened. A woman in the street asked Laura a
question in English. The woman was a tourist from Canada, and she wanted the
train station. Laura understood her, and she said: "Go down this street, then
turn left." The woman said "Thank you!" and Laura was very proud.

Next year Laura is going to travel to Canada. She is going to talk to people in
English every day.

"English is difficult," she says to her friend David. "But every new word is a
small gift."

## New Words

- **learn** — to get new knowledge in your head
- **study** — to read and work to learn something
- **student** — a person who learns at a school or a class
- **mistake** — something you do or say that is not right
- **difficult** — not easy
- **film** — a movie
- **tourist** — a person who travels to see a new place
- **proud** — very happy about something good you did
