# Learning English

My name is Yuki. I am 24 years old, and I am learning English.

I started my English classes six months ago. My teacher's name is Mr. Brown. He is from England, and he is very patient with us.

Every Tuesday and Thursday, I go to class after work. The class is from seven to nine in the evening. Sometimes I am tired after work, but I try to concentrate.

In class, we learn new words every day. We also practice speaking with other students. My classmates are from different countries: Brazil, China, and Poland. This is interesting because we learn about different cultures too.

At home, I study for thirty minutes every day. I use a phone app to practice. I also watch English videos with subtitles. This helps me a lot.

At first, English was very difficult for me. The grammar is different from my language. But now, after six months, I can understand more.

I can talk about my family, my job, and my daily routine. I can also read simple stories, like this one!

My favorite part of learning English is when I can talk to tourists in my city. Last week, a tourist from Canada asked me for directions. I understood her question, and I helped her. I felt very proud.

Learning a new language is not easy. Sometimes I make mistakes, and sometimes I forget words. But my teacher says mistakes are normal and important for learning.

My goal is to speak English very well next year. I want to travel to other countries and talk to people from all over the world.

Learning English is difficult, but it is also fun. Every new word is like a small gift.
