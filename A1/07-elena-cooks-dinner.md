# Elena Cooks Dinner

Tonight Elena is cooking dinner for two friends, Laura and David. She does this
one time every month.

Elena is not a very good cook, but she has one very good dinner: chicken with
rice. Her grandmother made this food for her when she was a child, and now
Elena makes it too.

At five o'clock she goes to the store. She buys chicken, rice, onions, tomatoes
and salt. She also buys a cake, because she does not make cakes.

At six o'clock she starts. First she cuts the onions and the tomatoes. Then she
puts the chicken in a big pan with oil. The kitchen is hot and it smells very
good.

"Now I wait twenty minutes," Elena says. She washes the plates and glasses and
she puts them on the table.

At seven o'clock she is ready. But then she looks at the rice — and there is no
rice in her apartment! She has chicken, but no rice.

Elena calls Laura.

"Laura, please, can you bring rice? I have no rice!"

Laura laughs. "Of course. No problem. I am near the store now."

Laura and David arrive at half past seven with the rice and some wine. Elena
cooks the rice in fifteen minutes, and at eight o'clock they eat.

"This is delicious!" David says. "What is in it?"

"Onion, tomato, a little salt — and it is my grandmother's dinner," Elena says.
"But today it is also Laura's dinner, because she brought the rice!"

They eat the cake, they drink coffee and they talk about their work and their
families. At midnight Laura and David go home.

## New Words

- **cut** — to make something in two parts with a knife
- **oil** — a liquid from plants that you use to cook
- **smell** — what your nose tells you about a thing
- **plate** — the flat thing you put your food on
- **glass** — you drink water or wine from it
- **wine** — a red or white drink with alcohol, from fruit
- **delicious** — very, very good to eat
- **midnight** — twelve o'clock at night
