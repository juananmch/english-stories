# A New Job

Today is Monday and it is David's first day in a new job. David is 31 years old.

He is nervous, but he is happy too. He wakes up early, at six o'clock. He takes
a shower and he eats bread and coffee. He does not eat a lot because he is
nervous.

David puts on a new white shirt and black pants. He looks at himself and he
says: "It is a good day. I can do this."

He leaves home at seven o'clock. The new office is in the center of the city,
so he takes the train.

The train is full of people. David stands near the door and he looks out of the
window.

He arrives at the office at eight o'clock. The building is very big and new.

A woman is waiting for him at the door.

"Good morning, David. My name is Sandra and I am your manager. Welcome!"

Sandra is friendly and she smiles a lot. She shows him the office. There are
many desks and computers. David's desk is next to a window, and he likes that.

Then Sandra says: "This is Laura, and this is Tom. You work with them."

"Hello!" Laura says. "Do you want a coffee?"

At one o'clock David eats lunch with Laura. They talk about their work and
their families. Laura is very friendly and David is not nervous now.

The first day is long and David is tired at five o'clock. But he is happy,
because his new job is good and the people are nice.

## New Words

- **nervous** — afraid about something before it happens
- **train** — it carries many people and it runs on two lines of metal
- **manager** — the person who says what the workers do
- **welcome** — a friendly word for a person who arrives
- **friendly** — nice to other people
- **smile** — to make a happy face with your mouth
- **desk** — the table where you work or write
- **computer** — a machine for work, email and the internet

## Questions

1. What time does David wake up?
2. How does David go to the new office?
3. Who is Sandra?
4. Where is David's desk?
5. Why is David happy at the end of the day?
