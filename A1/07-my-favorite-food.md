# My Favorite Food

Hello, my name is Fatima. I want to talk about food, because I love food very much!

My favorite meal is dinner. I like dinner because I can relax and eat slowly with my family.

My favorite dish is chicken soup. My grandmother makes the best chicken soup in the world. It has chicken, carrots, onions, and noodles. When I am sick, my mother always cooks this soup for me. It makes me feel better.

I also like pizza. On Friday nights, my family often orders pizza. We choose a pizza with cheese, tomato, and mushrooms. We eat it in front of the television. It is not very healthy, but it is tasty.

For breakfast, I like simple food. I eat toast with butter and jam. I also drink a cup of tea with milk. I don't like coffee; it is too strong for me.

At work, I usually eat a sandwich for lunch. My favorite sandwich has cheese, tomato, and lettuce. Sometimes I also eat an apple or a banana.

I don't like spicy food. When food is too spicy, I can't eat it. My husband loves spicy food, so this is a problem in our kitchen! He puts hot sauce on everything.

On my birthday, I always want chocolate cake. My sister makes a chocolate cake for me every year. It is the best cake I know.

I think food is not just about eating. Food is about family, memories, and love. When I cook my grandmother's soup, I remember her kitchen and her smile.

What is your favorite food?
