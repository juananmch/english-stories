# The Weekend

Finally, it is Saturday! David loves the weekend because he doesn't work.

He wakes up late, at ten o'clock. This is very different from weekdays, when he wakes up at six thirty.

He makes a big breakfast: eggs, toast, and orange juice. He eats slowly and reads the news on his phone.

At eleven, his friend Jack calls him. "Do you want to play tennis?" asks Jack.

"Yes, of course!" says David.

They meet at the sports center at twelve o'clock. They play tennis for one hour. David is not very good at tennis, but he has fun. Jack wins the game, but David doesn't mind.

After tennis, they are hungry. They go to a pizza restaurant. They order a big pizza with cheese and vegetables. They also drink cold soda.

"This is a perfect Saturday," says David.

In the afternoon, David goes home. He cleans his apartment and washes his clothes. He doesn't like these jobs, but his home needs to be clean.

At six o'clock, he calls his girlfriend, Carmen. They plan to watch a movie tonight.

Carmen arrives at seven thirty with popcorn. They choose an action movie. The movie is exciting, with a lot of action.

After the movie, they cook dinner together. They make pasta with tomato sauce. It is simple, but delicious.

On Sunday, David relaxes at home. He doesn't do very much. He listens to music and calls his mother.

He knows that on Monday, he will work again. But today, the weekend is still here, and he wants to enjoy every minute of it.
