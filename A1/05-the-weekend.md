# The Weekend

It is Saturday, and David loves the weekend because he does not work.

He wakes up late, at ten o'clock. On other days he wakes up at six, so ten
o'clock is very good for him.

He makes a big breakfast: eggs, bread and orange juice. He eats slowly and he
reads the news on his phone.

At eleven o'clock his friend Carlos calls him.

"Hello, David! Do you want to play tennis today?"

"Yes! Of course," David says.

They meet at the park at twelve o'clock. They play tennis for one hour. Carlos
is very good, and he wins the game. David is not very good at tennis, but he
likes it a lot.

After the game they are hungry, so they go to a small restaurant near the park.
They eat a big pizza with cheese and tomato. They drink cold water because it is
a hot day.

"This is a perfect Saturday," David says.

In the afternoon David is at home. He cleans his apartment and he washes his
clothes. This is not fun, but his apartment is dirty and he has time now.

At seven o'clock Elena comes to his apartment. They are going to watch a movie
together. Elena brings popcorn.

The movie is very long, but it is good. After the movie they cook pasta and
they talk about their week.

On Sunday David is at home all day. He listens to music, he reads a book and he
calls his mother.

Tomorrow is Monday and he is going to work again. But now it is Sunday
afternoon, and the weekend is not finished.

## New Words

- **weekend** — Saturday and Sunday
- **late** — after the normal time
- **park** — a big green place in a city with trees and grass
- **tennis** — a game for two people with a small ball
- **win** — to be number one in a game
- **game** — something you play, like tennis or soccer
- **movie** — a long story with pictures that you watch
- **popcorn** — white food from corn that you eat at the movies

## Questions

2. Who calls David at eleven o'clock?
3. Who wins the tennis game?
4. What does David do in the afternoon?
5. What do David and Elena do at seven o'clock?
