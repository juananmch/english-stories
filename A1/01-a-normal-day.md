# A Normal Day

My name is Laura. I am 28 years old. I live in a small apartment in the city.

Every day, I wake up at seven o'clock. I drink coffee and eat bread with cheese. Then I take a shower and get dressed.

I go to work by bus. The bus is often full of people. I work in an office. I am a secretary. My job is not very exciting, but I like my co-workers.

At one o'clock, I have lunch with my friend Maria. We eat in a small restaurant near the office. The food is good and not expensive.

I finish work at five o'clock. I walk home because I like fresh air. On the way, I buy milk and eggs at the store.

At home, I cook dinner. I like simple food: rice, chicken, and vegetables. After dinner, I watch television or read a book.

I go to bed at eleven o'clock. Tomorrow is a new day, but it is almost the same as today. I don't mind. I like my quiet life.

On Friday, everything is different. I finish work early. I meet my friends at a café. We talk and laugh for hours. I love Fridays.

My life is simple, but I am happy. I have a job, good friends, and a nice home. What more do I need?

## New Words

wake up, shower, get dressed, bus, office, co-worker, lunch, restaurant, quiet
