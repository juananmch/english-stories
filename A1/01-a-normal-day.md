# A Normal Day

My name is Laura. I am 28 years old. I live in a small apartment in the city.

Every day I wake up at seven o'clock. I drink coffee and I eat bread with
cheese. Then I take a shower and I get dressed.

I go to work by bus. The bus is always full of people. I work in an office in
the center of the city.

At one o'clock I eat lunch with my friend Elena. We go to a small restaurant
near the office. The food is good and it is not expensive.

I finish work at five o'clock. I walk home because I like the fresh air. On the
way I buy milk and eggs at the store.

At home I cook dinner. I like simple food: rice, chicken and vegetables. After
dinner I read a book or I watch television.

I go to bed at eleven o'clock. Tomorrow is a new day, but it is almost the same
as today. I am happy with my quiet life.

## New Words

- **wake up** — to stop sleeping in the morning
- **shower** — you stand under water to get clean
- **get dressed** — to put your clothes on
- **office** — the room or building where people do office work
- **lunch** — the food you eat in the middle of the day
- **restaurant** — a place where you pay for food and eat it
- **fresh** — new and clean, like good air outside
- **quiet** — with little noise

## Questions

1. What time does Laura wake up?
2. How does Laura go to work?
3. Who does Laura eat lunch with?
4. Why does Laura walk home?
5. What does Laura do after dinner?
