# A Trip to the City

Sara and her husband, Luis, want a short trip. They decide to visit a new city for the weekend.

On Friday evening, they pack their bags. They put in clothes, shoes, and a camera. Sara is very excited because she loves to travel.

On Saturday morning, they take a train to the city. The journey takes two hours. They talk and look out of the window during the trip.

When they arrive, they find their hotel. It is a small hotel near the center. Their room is not big, but it is clean and comfortable.

First, they walk around the old part of the city. There are many old buildings and small streets. They take a lot of photos.

At one o'clock, they are hungry. They find a nice restaurant. Sara orders fish with salad. Luis orders soup and bread. The food is delicious.

After lunch, they visit a museum. The museum has old paintings and interesting objects from the past. Luis likes history, so he reads every sign carefully. Sara prefers to look at the paintings.

In the afternoon, they walk to a big park. The park has a lake with ducks. They sit on a bench and rest their legs.

"I love this city," says Sara.

"Me too," says Luis. "We should come back next year."

In the evening, they find a small bar with live music. They drink lemonade and listen to the music. It is a wonderful evening.

On Sunday, before they go home, they buy some souvenirs for their family: a magnet for the fridge and some local candy.

They take the train home in the afternoon. They are tired, but very happy. It was a perfect short trip.
