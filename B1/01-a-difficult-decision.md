# A Difficult Decision

Sophie had worked as a nurse for nine years, and for most of that time she had
loved her job. Lately, though, something had changed. The hospital where she
worked was short of staff, and she was doing twelve-hour shifts three or four
times a week.

One evening, her friend Marcus called her.

"You sound exhausted," he said. "Are you okay?"

"Honestly? I don't know," Sophie admitted. "I keep thinking about quitting."

"And doing what instead?"

"There's a training course for physiotherapists. It would take two years, and
I would have almost no income while I studied."

Marcus was quiet for a moment. "That's a huge risk. But if you hate your job
now, it might get worse, not better."

Sophie had considered this before, but hearing someone else say it made the
decision feel more real. She spent the next few weeks weighing the options.
Her savings would cover about a year without a full salary. Her partner,
Daniel, offered to take on more of their expenses if she needed it.

"I don't want you to feel pressured," Daniel told her. "But I also don't want
you to stay somewhere that's making you miserable, just because it feels
safer."

That comment stayed with her. She realized she had been assuming that leaving
nursing would be a failure, when actually it might just be a change.

In March, Sophie applied for the course. She was nervous while she waited to
hear back, checking her email far more often than usual. When the acceptance
letter finally arrived, she read it three times before she believed it.

Her last shift at the hospital was strange. Colleagues she had worked beside
for years wished her luck, and one of them admitted that she was thinking of
doing something similar herself.

"If it doesn't work out," Marcus told her later, "you can always come back to
nursing."

"I know," Sophie said. "But I don't think I will need to."

Two years later, she was seeing her first patients as a qualified
physiotherapist. It hadn't been easy, and there had been moments when she
doubted herself completely. Still, whenever she remembered that phone call
with Marcus, she felt grateful she had taken that chance.

## New Words

- **quit** — to stop doing a job or an activity
- **weigh** — to think carefully about the good and bad points of something
- **expense** — money that has to be spent on something
- **pressured** — made to feel that you must do something
- **miserable** — very unhappy
- **acceptance** — being told officially that you can join something
- **qualified** — having passed the training needed for a job
